Identifying Dry Skin and its Routine

Dry skin often feels tight, rough, or flaky, especially after cleansing. It may appear dull and lack natural glow because it does not retain enough moisture. Pores are usually small and less visible, but the skin can feel dehydrated throughout the day. Makeup may cling to dry patches and look uneven. This skin type needs consistent hydration and protection of the skin barrier.

Focus: Hydration and barrier repair

Make sure to avoid harsh cleansers or strong chemical cleansers that can over-strip the skin. Also avoid frequent exfoliation, which can remove healthy skin cells that have not fully developed. Focus on layering hydrating products.

Step 1: Oil cleanser

Use nourishing plant-based oils instead of oils with heavy fragrances.

Example :

  • Ma:nyo Pure Cleanser Oil

Step 2: Water Cleanser (AM + PM, Essential)

Use a low pH, gel-based gentle cleanser that does not strip the skin’s natural oils. Also avoid using foaming or sulfate-based cleansers,as they can dry out your skin even more skin.

Example:

  • COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser

Step 3: Exfoliator (Skip or 1x/week only)

Dry skin should be exfoliated very gently and rarely. Over exfoliating can worsen dryness and irritation. Use mild acids only when needed.

Example:

  • Mediheal Phyto-Enzyme Peeling Pads

Step 4: Hydrating Toner (AM + PM, Essential)

Use hydrating toners with ingredients like rice extract, glycerin, or hyaluronic acid. Apply 2 layers for extra hydration.

Example:

  • I'm From Rice Toner/

Step 5/6: Essence and Serums/Ampoules

These skincare steps vary in skin concern other than your skin type.

Examples for hydration focus:

  • Torriden Dive-In Hyaluronic Acid Serum - offers intense hydration
  • >PDRN Hyaluronic Acid Capsule 100 Serum - imrpoves skin elasticity

Step 7: Sheet Mask

Provides a quick boost of hydration when the skin feels dry or dull. Sheet masks are soaked in essence that helps refresh and soften the skin.

Example:

  • Dr.Jart+ Dermask Soothing Hydra Solution Sheet Mask

Step 8: Eye Cream

This step hydrates the delicate under-eye area and helps prevent fine lines caused by dryness.

Example:

  • Skin1004 Madagascar Centella Probio-Cica Bakuchiol Eye Cream

Step 9: Moisturizer

This step locks in all previous layers and prevents moisture loss. You should use rich creams with ceramides, or shea butter and avoid gel-only moisturizers that may not provide enough hydration.

Example:

  • Torriden DIVE IN Hyaluronic Acid Soothing Cream

Step 10: Sunscreen

This step protects the skin from UV damage, premature aging, and dark spots. You should use moisturizing sunscreens that do not dry out the skin.

Example:

  • Round Lab Birch Juice Sunscreen
