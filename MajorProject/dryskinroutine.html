Dry Skin

Dry skin often feels tight, rough, or flaky, especially after cleansing. It may appear dull and lack natural glow because it does not retain enough moisture. Pores are usually small and less visible, but the skin can feel dehydrated throughout the day. Makeup may cling to dry patches and look uneven. This skin type needs consistent hydration and protection of the skin barrier.

Focus: Hydration and barrier repair

Make sure to avoid harsh cleansers or strong chemical cleansers that can over-strip the skin. Also avoid frequent exfoliation, which can remove healthy skin cells that have not fully developed. Focus on layering hydrating products.

Step 1: Oil Cleanser (PM, Recommended)

This step gently removes sunscreen and impurities without stripping moisture. Use nourishing plant-based oils instead of oils with heavy fragrances.
