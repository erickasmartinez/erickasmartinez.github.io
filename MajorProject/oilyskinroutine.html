Identifying Oily Skin and its Routine

Oily skin produces excess sebrum, especially in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin). The skin often looks shiny throughout the day and may feel greasy shortly after cleansing. Pores are also usually more visible, and breakouts or blackheads are common. The goal for this skin type is balance, not removing all oil.

Focus: Oil control and lightweight hydration

Use lightweight products with ingredients like niacinamide, green tea, and salicylic acid. Avoid heavy oils, thick creams, and over-cleansing, because this can trigger more oil production.

Step 1: Oil Cleanser

Use lightweight oils such as grapeseed or centella-based formulas.

Example:

  • Beauty of Joseon Ginseng Cleansing Oil

Step 2: Water-Based Cleanser

Use cleansers that focus on balencing hydration and reducing sebrum production. They can be gel based due to there lightweigth and foaming formulas.

Example:

  • Anua Heartleaf Quercetinol Pore Deep Cleansing Foam

Step 3: Exfoliator

Use BHA (salicylic acid) to target oil inside pores to help unclog them.

Example:

  • Some By Mi AHA BHA PHA Toner

Step 4: Toner

Use toners with niacinamide, green tea, or centella and avoid alcohol-heavy toners that dry out the skin

Example:

  • Anua Heartleaf 77% Soothing Toner

Step 5/6: Essence and Serums/Ampoules

Use niacinamide or zinc-based serums. These skincare steps vary in skin concern other than your skin type

Examples for decreasing sebrum production:

  • Numbuzin No.3 Serum
  • Skin1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule

Step 7: Sheet Mask

Use lightweight masks with tea tree or centella.

Example:

  • Mediheal Tea Tree Mask

Step 8: Eye Cream

This step hydrates the under-eye area without heaviness, you should use lightweight formulas.

Example:

  • Etude SoonJung Eye Cream

Step 9: Moisturizer

This step hydrates the skin while controlling oil balance you should use gel-based moisturizers with lightweight textures.

Example:

  • illiyoon ceramide ato soothing gel cream

Step 10: Sunscreen

Avoid greasy or heavy SPF formulas.

Example:

  • Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun SPF50
