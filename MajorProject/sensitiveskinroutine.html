Identifying Sensitive Skin and its Routine

Sensitive skin reacts easily to products, weather changes, and environmental stress. It may show redness, burning, or irritation. The skin barrier is often weakened, so gentle care is essential. The goal is to calm, protect, and strengthen the skin.

Focus: Barrier repair and calming care

Use fragrance-free, minimal-ingredient products. Avoid strong acids, alcohol, and essential oils. Keep the routine simple to reduce irritation.

Step 1: Oil Cleanser

Gently removes sunscreen and impurities without irritating sensitive skin.

Example:

  • Hada Labo Gokujyun Cleansing Oil

Step 2: Water-Based Cleanser

Cleans the skin without disrupting the natural barrier or causing dryness.

Example:

  • cosrx low ph best water cleanser

Step 3: Exfoliator

Only use occasionally with very mild formulas to avoid irritation and redness.

Example:

  • Neogen Bio-Peel Gauze Peeling Pads (Green Tea)

Step 4: Toner

Calms the skin and reduces irritation while restoring hydration after cleansing.

Example:

  • Isntree Hyaluronic Acid Toner

Step 5/6: Essence and Serums/Ampoules

Strengthens the skin barrier and reduces sensitivity using calming ingredients.

Example:

  • Purito Centella Unscented Serum

Step 7: Sheet Mask

Soothes irritation and provides gentle hydration without stressing the skin.

Example:

  • Benton Aloe Soothing Mask Pack

Step 8: Eye Cream

Hydrates sensitive eye area while preventing irritation and dryness.

Example:

  • Klairs Fundamental Eye Awakening Gel

Step 9: Moisturizer

Repairs and strengthens the skin barrier while locking in moisture.

Example:

  • Dr. Jart+ Cicapair Cream

Step 10: Sunscreen

Protects sensitive skin from UV damage without causing irritation or stinging.

Example:

  • Etude SoonJung Sun Milk SPF50
