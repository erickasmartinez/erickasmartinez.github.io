Identifying Combination Skin and its Routine

Combination skin includes both oily and dry areas. The T-zone is usually oily, while the cheeks may feel dry or normal. This creates uneven texture and requires a balanced skincare approach. The goal is to control oil without drying out the skin.

Focus:Balance and targeted hydration

Use lightweight hydration for oily areas and richer moisture for dry areas. Avoid overly heavy creams or harsh cleansers that disrupt balance.

Step 1: Oil Cleanser

Removes sunscreen and excess oil while keeping the skin balanced and comfortable.

Example:

  • Haruharu wonder black rice moisture deep cleansing oil

Step 2: Water Based Cleanser

Gently cleanses without stripping moisture, helping both oily and dry areas stay balanced.

Example:

  • Etude SoonJung Whip Cleanser

Step 3: Exfoliator

Helps remove buildup in oily areas while keeping dry areas protected from irritation.

Example:

  • Isntree Chestnut BHA 2% Clear Liquid

Step 4: Toner

Restores hydration and helps balance uneven skin zones after cleansing.

Example:

  • Round Lab Mugwort Calming Toner

Step 5/6: Essence and Serums/Ampoules

Targets different needs such as hydration in dry areas and oil control in the T-zone.

Example:

  • Missha Time Revolution First Essence

Step 7: Sheet Mask

Provides lightweight hydration without overwhelming oily areas.

Example:

  • Dr. Jart+ Vital Hydra Solution Mask

Step 8: Eye Cream

Hydrates delicate skin around the eyes without adding heaviness.

Example:

  • Laneige Water Bank Eye Gel

Step 9: Moisturizer

Balances hydration by providing moisture without clogging pores.

Example:

  • Dr. G Green Mild Up Moisturizer

Step 10: Sunscreen

Protects all skin areas while maintaining a light, non-greasy finish.

Example:

  • Missha All Around Safe Block Essence Sun SPF45
